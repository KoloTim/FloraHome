📖 Quick guide

1 · Watering your plant

“💧 Water” runs as a single dose (default ~20 s, hard-capped at 45 s on the device). For automatic watering: Settings → Watering.

2 · Calibrating soil moisture

Probe in air → enter the raw voltage on the left; probe in a glass of water → value on the right. “Apply values” sends the calibration to the device (it stays saved).

3 · Setting up a plant

“Edit profile” → choose the species (soil moisture, temperature and light ranges are applied) and set nickname/emoji/colour.

4 · Adding a new node

deploy/add-node.sh new plant-d "Monstera"
deploy/add-node.sh discover        # welcher ESP auf welchem Port
deploy/add-node.sh compile plant-d
deploy/add-node.sh flash  plant-d /dev/ttyUSB0
deploy/add-node.sh ota    plant-d 10.42.0.x

OTA also works from the browser: ESPHome on :6052. Full guides: TUTORIALS.md in the repo.
