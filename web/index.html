🪴 Plant profile

📖 How it works

1 · Sign in

Sign in at the top right — it unlocks adding, editing and deleting plants, watering and flashing.

2 · Add a plant from a photo

Plants → “📷 New plant”: give it a name and take/choose a photo. The AI identifies the species and proposes the natural care values — confirm to create the profile.

3 · Assign it to an ESP board

In the same dialog pick the board this plant lives on. plant-a / plant-b / plant-c are the board profiles in the ESPHome folder. Not flashed yet? Flash it under Backend → Devices & flashing (or ESPHome on :6052) — the plant then appears with live data as soon as that board comes online.

4 · Manage & fine-tune

Every plant card has ✏️ Edit and 🗑️ Delete. Set watering and light under Control → ⚙️ Watering, and choose how much FlorAI may change.

Everything runs on the Pi already — no PC needed. Full guides live in the repo's docs/ folder.
